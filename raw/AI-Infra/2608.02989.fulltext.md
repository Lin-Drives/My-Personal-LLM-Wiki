Related Work
Speculative Decoding
Speculative decoding uses a draft mechanism to propose
a block of tokens that the target model verifies in one
parallel forward pass, with an accept/reject rule that pre-

also chooses one expert set for a parallel workload. Accept-
MoE specifically uses the position-dependent commitment

Speculative Decoding for MoE Models
A recent line of work studies how speculation interacts with
MoE sparsity. MoESD analyzes how batch size and sparsity
affecttargetefficiencyandspeculativespeedup(Huangetal.
2026b), whereas Cascade measures speculation utility on-

Under expert offloading, SP-MoE uses draft-model atten-

target verification. EVICT selects an ancestor-closed prefix

cost (Pan et al. 2026). EcoSpec addresses expert scattering
