When several completions repeat the same underlying solu-

proportion to its observed multiplicity, whereas a rare cor-
rectformreceivesonlythecreditofitsfewinstances.GRPO
is therefore uniform at the completion level but frequency-
skewed after credit is aggregated by recurring structure. We
refer to this behavior as multiplicity-induced structure-level
credit concentration.
This allocation is consistent with expected-reward op-
timization, but it is poorly aligned with high-budget re-
peated sampling, where retaining alternative correct modes
can improve the probability of solving difficult problems.
Priorworkaddressesthissettingthroughentropyregulariza-

partition construction and positive-credit redistribution are
twodistinctdesigncomponents.Thecentralquestionishow

pletions has been partitioned, and whether a useful partition
can be obtained without full semantic strategy recovery.
We address this question by introducing Cue-GRPO, a
low-overhead method that separates partition construction
from credit allocation. At its core, a partition-conditioned
Credit Redistribution (CR) rule rebalances positive advan-

applies finite-group stabilization to the redistributed coef-
ficients. In this design, CR determines how credit enters
the policy update, while Strategy Cues provide the partition

RLVRunderrepeatedsamplinganddiversity-preservingop-

A related line of work explicitly preserves exploration

methodsencouragebroadertoken-levelsupport(Chengetal.
2026; Huang et al. 2026), pass@k-aware objectives di-

verification: group-relative advantages treat correct comple-

same solution structure. Cue-GRPO therefore complements
exploration-oriented methods by redistributing credit within
the set of already verified-correct completions, without di-

Rarity-aware credit redistribution.DARLING (Li et al.
2025) groups responses through learned semantic partitions
